Step 2: Select Your Job

Your Job defines your combat role and abilities. Jobs are grouped by role: Tank, Healer, DPS (Melee, Ranged Physical, Ranged Magical).

Tank Jobs

  • Paladin: Holy sword-and-shield protector (Gladiator base)
  • Warrior: Berserker with massive axe (Marauder base)
  • Dark Knight: Darkness-wielding greatsword user
  • Gunbreaker: Gunblade-wielding defender

Healer Jobs

  • White Mage: Pure healing magic (Conjurer base)
  • Scholar: Strategic healer with fairy companion (Arcanist base)
  • Astrologian: Fortune-reading celestial healer
  • Sage: Barrier-focused tech healer

DPS Jobs (Melee)

  • Monk: Martial artist with chakra (Pugilist base)
  • Dragoon: Dragon-empowered lancer (Lancer base)
  • Ninja: Stealth assassin with ninjutsu (Rogue base)
  • Samurai: Eastern swordmaster with kenki
  • Reaper: Scythe-wielder who bargains with a Voidsent

DPS Jobs (Ranged Physical)

  • Bard: Archer who sings battle songs (Archer base)
  • Machinist: Gadgeteer with firearms and robots
  • Dancer: Elegant fighter who buffs allies

DPS Jobs (Ranged Magical)

  • Black Mage: Destructive elemental magic (Thaumaturge base)
  • Summoner: Calls forth primals/egi (Arcanist base)
  • Red Mage: Balanced spellblade with dual magic
  • Blue Mage: Learns enemy skills (Limited Job)

Step 3: Assign Attributes

Your character has six primary attributes. Assign points based on your Job's needs.

Attribute Description Used By
Strength (STR) Physical power Tanks, Melee DPS
Dexterity (DEX) Agility and precision Ninja, Bard, Machinist
Vitality (VIT) Health and endurance All roles (survivability)
Intelligence (INT) Magical knowledge Magical DPS (BLM, SMN, RDM)
Mind (MND) Spiritual power Healers (WHM, SCH, AST, SGE)
Charisma (CHA) Force of personality Social encounters, Bard
Use point-buy or standard array to assign attributes. Your Race provides bonuses to specific attributes. Prioritize your Job's primary stat (STR for tanks/melee, DEX for physical ranged, INT for magical DPS, MND for healers).

Step 4: Join a Grand Company

The Three Great City-States each have a Grand Company — military organizations that defend Eorzea. Choose your allegiance.

The Three Grand Companies

  • Maelstrom (Limsa Lominsa): Naval power, pirates and sailors
  • Order of the Twin Adder (Gridania): Forest protectors, nature and balance
  • Immortal Flames (Ul'dah): Desert warriors, merchants and wealth

Step 6: Acquire Equipment

Your starting gear depends on your Job. Tanks need armor, DPS need weapons, Healers need focus items.

Starting Equipment by Role

  • Tanks: Heavy armor, shield, weapon (sword/axe/greatsword/gunblade)
  • Healers: Light robes, focus item (cane/codex/star globe/nouliths)
  • Melee DPS: Medium armor, melee weapon (fists/lance/daggers/katana/scythe)
  • Ranged Physical: Light armor, ranged weapon (bow/gun/chakrams)
  • Ranged Magical: Cloth robes, magical focus (staff/grimoire/rapier/cane)

Step 7: Game Mechanics

D20 SYSTEM
When you attempt an action, roll 1d20 and add your attribute modifier and proficiency bonus. If your total meets or exceeds the Difficulty Class (DC), you succeed!

Natural 20: Critical success!
Natural 1: Critical failure!

Core Mechanics

  • Hit Points (HP): Determined by Job and VIT
  • Mana Points (MP): For magical abilities
  • Limit Break: Party resource for powerful abilities
  • Job Gauge: Class-specific resource mechanics
  • Global Cooldown (GCD): Action timing mechanic

Step 8: The Echo

As a Warrior of Light, you possess the Echo — a mysterious power that lets you transcend death, see visions, and resist primal influence.

The Echo grants:
• Resistance to primal tempering
• Visions of past and future
• Ability to overcome death (revive)
• Understanding of all languages
• Connection to Hydaelyn (the Mothercrystal)

This power marks you as a hero of destiny.
